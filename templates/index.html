08 A systematic approach

Debugging workflow.

A concise view of the investigation methods and tools used in platform validation.

    Make it repeatable

    Capture the failing configuration and the steps that trigger the issue.

  2. 02 / COLLECT

    Gather evidence

    Review logs and traces alongside platform behavior.

  3. 03 / ISOLATE

    Trace the failure

    Use JTAG, ITP, and logic analyzers to narrow the problem area.

  4. 04 / VERIFY

    Prove the fix

    Rerun the focused test and regression coverage on the target setup.
